Welcome to OpenBass! This is my first DIY guitar project. There are many
3D printed guitar projects online, but not many 3D printed basses with
fully 3D printed guitar necks. I saw this as an opportunity to push myself
as a designer and see what the limitations were with this kind of project.
This was a great hands-on experience with design intent and
parametrically driven models.
Like many of my projects, I wanted a headless, multiscale, 4-string bass
and building one was a great way to customize it to my exact
tbenen

I used Matte PLA to keep it minimal, but the model lends itself to use all
sorts of fun filament colors for the body. Includes 3D printed fret press and
3D printed fret bender!
For the full design guide, parts list, and assembly instructions, see the Doc
here or in PDF in files: https://docs.google.com/document/d/
1787XH67hc2K-ue8DQ6k_0tpD97aBMxZDR4CtvU6GlKI/edit?usp=sharing
8/9/25: Added Pickup_Placement_Ratio parameter for angled pickups,
suggested by u/Poulito on r/Luthier
10/10/25: There was a mistake with the V2.1 neck models that made the
fret slots too shallow close to the body. This has been updated in the
model, but if you downloaded before today you can deepen the fretslots
with a hacksaw! If the frets end up a bit loose due to the widened slot, you
can add superglue to ensure it seats permanently. 
9/10/26: Ploutone no longer makes the headless bridges used in the
original models. There may be remixes for alternate part compatibility!
